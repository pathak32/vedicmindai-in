Privacy Policy — VedicMind

Last Updated: June 12, 2025

VedicMind ("we","our","us") operates the VedicMind app at https://vedicmindai.in. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

  • Name and mobile number (account creation)
  • Age, grade, learning goal (onboarding profile)
  • Learning progress, quiz scores, XP, streaks
  • Payment status via Razorpay (we never store card details)
  • Anonymous usage analytics via Google Analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • Personalise your Vedic Mathematics learning path
  • Track progress and generate performance reports
  • Process subscription payments
  • Improve app features using anonymous analytics

We never sell your data. VedicMind is completely ad-free.

Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude API — AI tutor (no PII sent)
  • Razorpay — Payment processing
  • Google Analytics — Anonymous usage tracking
  • Supabase — Database (Mumbai, India region)

Children's Privacy

VedicMind serves students of all ages including children under 13. Parents or guardians must create accounts for children under 13. We collect only data necessary to provide the learning service. We do not share children's data with third parties for commercial purposes. To request deletion of a child's data, email hitesh@vedicmindai.in.

Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on Supabase servers in Mumbai, India. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, go to Profile > Delete Account in the app, or email hitesh@vedicmindai.in with subject "Account Deletion Request". Data will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of significant changes via in-app notification and by updating the date above.
